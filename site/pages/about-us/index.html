Our Story

50+ Years of Innovation
& Excellence

For over five decades, INALSA has been a trusted name in Indian homes, delivering innovative and reliable home appliances that simplify everyday living. Established in India during the 1960s and strengthened through global expertise under the Taurus Group since 2004, INALSA has grown into one of the country’s leading mass-premium home appliance brands.

From pioneering the food processor category in India to expanding into modern kitchen and home care solutions, INALSA has continuously evolved with the changing needs of consumers. Our extensive product portfolio includes food processors, mixer grinders, air fryers, OTGs, stand mixers, vacuum cleaners, garment steamers, coffee makers, pressure washers, hand blenders, electric kettles, and much more.

At INALSA, we believe great products should combine international quality, smart innovation, and affordability. This philosophy has helped us build the trust of over 10 million households across India. Our strong omnichannel presence spans over 25,000 retail outlets, major e-commerce platforms, institutional channels, and service coverage across 15,000+ pin codes nationwide.

Driven by a lean and agile team, INALSA remains committed to delivering durable products, dependable service, and meaningful customer experiences. As we continue to expand through new product innovations and upcoming experience centres across India, our focus remains the same — making modern living smarter, easier, and more accessible for every Indian home.
